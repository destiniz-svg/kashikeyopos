Everything a floor runs on

One system, from the QR on the table to the trial balance.

Not a till with apps bolted on — one chain of consequence: ticket → sale → payment → tax → stock → ledger → settlement, in a single transaction.

⚡
offline-first

A dropped connection never drops a sale

Every action queues durably on the till and replays exactly once when the link returns. Take orders, settle bills and print receipts through a full outage — the outbox holds until the network is back, and a replay can never double-charge.

📱
guest QR

Guests order from the table

Each table's QR mints a token scoped to that one table — a guest can't wander into another table's bill. Orders land on the till and the kitchen display instantly, and the guest tracks their food from "in the kitchen" to "ready at the pass".

🧾

GST the way MIRA expects

GGST or TGST from your own effective-dated rate. Not registered? No tax line prints at all — and the system watches your rolling turnover against the MVR 1,000,000 threshold for you.

📒

Real double-entry accounts

Every sale posts its full journal — revenue, tax, service, COGS, tender. An unbalanced entry is refused by the database itself. Your P&L is a fact, not a hope.

💳

Settlement that ties to the bank

Card, wallet and QR each carry their own contract — rate, cycle, evidence. Daily batches reconcile against the advice file, and a half-percent overcharge has nowhere to hide.

⭐

Loyalty guests can check

Points, tiers and receipts on the member's own phone at your store's address. Tiers are derived live from the ladder you publish — no stale "Platinum" from last year.

🖨️

Bytes on paper

ESC/POS dockets over USB, serial or the kitchen's LAN printer. The cash drawer opens on cash — and only on cash. A job with no printer says "spooled", never pretends.

🏝️

Your store's own address

your-store.kashikeyopos.com on every table card. Rename later and the old address redirects for ever — a printed QR outlives any decision.

floor · pass · office

Waiter, counter, kitchen and back office — one bill, four screens

A tablet takes the order, the pass fires it, the counter settles it, the office reads the day — the open ticket lives with the outlet, not trapped on one device. Split bills remember every share's tender; tips are held for staff, never absorbed into revenue; five staff ranks gate every screen, enforced by the database underneath the app.
